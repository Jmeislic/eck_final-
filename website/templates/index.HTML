In an effort to improve the moral reasoning of LLM outputs, we have created a new machine learning model, EthicAI. This model takes in a sentence and outputs its moral classification and a breakdown of why that decision was made. The model is composed of two different machine learning algorithms, a BART for text generation and then a BERT for moral classification. The BART is trained with an input of explanation section, and output the action, norm, intention and situation. The BERT is trained on action, norm, intention, and situation, and then outputs the label (which is a binary classification of either moral or immoral). Finally with these two parts combined the model can take in any sentence and parses out the different ethical components, (the action, norm, intention, and situation), and then inputs those into the BERT model for moral classification. For short sentences the BART does not have enough text to compose the full components of the sentence so there will not be an output of the action, norm, intention, and situation for sentences under 65 characters.
